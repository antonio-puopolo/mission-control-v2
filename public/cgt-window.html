What changed — Budget night 12 May 2026

The 50% discount is gone. But not yet.

Since 1999, any asset held for more than 12 months received a flat 50% discount on capital gains — meaning you only paid tax on half your profit. The 2026 Federal Budget scraps this entirely from 1 July 2027.

The new system taxes your real gain — that is, your profit above inflation — at your full marginal rate, with a minimum 30% tax floor. For most property investors with long-held assets, this means significantly more tax.
